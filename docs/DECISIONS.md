# Product Decisions

## D001 — Repository
All development for this product is performed in `lukeniukrostyslav/priduct`.

## D002 — Commercial direction
The product targets the digital-product creator market and will initially be packaged for Gumroad.

## D003 — Product model
Use a free entry product plus paid Core and Pro tiers.

## D004 — No guaranteed-income claims
Marketing must describe capabilities and workflows, not promise earnings.

## D005 — MVP before scale
Build and validate a small useful product before expanding into a large system.

## D006 — Continuous GitHub checkpoints
Every meaningful production block must end with a GitHub commit and a documented status.


## D007 — No Notion
LAUNCH OS will NOT include a Notion workspace, Notion companion, Notion database, or Notion integration. The product must stand on its own as an independent web application and downloadable/local assets where useful.

## D008 — Localization
The commercial product will support five languages:
- English (EN)
- Italian (IT)
- German (DE)
- French (FR)
- Russian (RU)

English is the primary commercial language. Localization must be treated as a product feature, not as machine-translated filler.

## D009 — Pricing strategy
Initial pricing will use a Free + Core + Pro structure. Prices are hypotheses to validate against product completeness and buyer response, not promises of value or sales.
